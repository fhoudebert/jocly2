X Men‘s Morris rules apply

To Win

Leave your opponent with less than 3 men, or without any possibility to play his turn.

To Play

Each time you align 3 of your men, you can remove one of your opponent's men from the game.

X represents the number of pieces (=men) you start with.

There are two phases in the game:

  • placing the men: at each turn you put one men on an available position (=all intersections and corners)
  • moving men: this phase starts when all the men are on the board. At your turn move one men from its position to an adjacent available position.

Standard or Fly

Nine and Twelve Men's Morris open by asking which of two rule sets you want to play. The choice counts as the first move of the game, so a recorded game replays under the rules it was played with.

  • Standard: men always move to an adjacent position, and a man that stands in a mill cannot be removed.
  • Fly: when you are reduced to 3 men you may move a man to any free position, not only an adjacent one. A man standing in a mill can also be removed in this variant.

Six and Seven Men's Morris are always played under the standard rules.
