Le Jeu de la guerre (A Game of War) was devised by Guy Debord, who registered its principle in 1965; he published it with Alice Becker-Ho (Le Jeu de la guerre : relevé des positions successives de toutes les forces au cours d'une partie, 1987).

The rules followed here are Debord's, read in the English translation published by Atlas Press (A Game of War, translated by Donald Nicholson-Smith). Where they leave a point open, this module follows the reading of Kriegspiel, the digital adaptation by the Radical Software Group (Alexander R. Galloway), whose rules text is released under a Creative Commons BY-NC-SA licence.

Neither text is reproduced: the rules pages of this module were written for Jocly. The free projects pykrieg and a-game-of-war (both GPL-3.0) were consulted for comparison; no code was taken from them.
