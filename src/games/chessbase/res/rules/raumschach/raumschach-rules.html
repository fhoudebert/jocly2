Differences from orthodox chess

Inital setup

Played on 5 5x5 boards, each player starts with 10 Pawns, 2 Rooks, 2 Bishops, 2 Knights, 2 Unicorns, 1 Queen and 1 King.

Pieces

Pawn

White pawns globaly move forward and upward, black pawns move forward and downward. Promotion rank is A1 for Black, E5 for White.

See illustration below for moves and captures.

Unicorn

Knight

Bishop

Rook

Queen

The Queen moves like Rook + Bishop + Unicorn.

King

The King moves like the Queen but limited to one square.

Features

Castling: no.

"En passant" capture: no.

Double move for Pawns (start): no.
