Kōtaishi Shogi

Kōtaishi Shogi is standard Shogi in which the extra piece is a Drunk Elephant (酔象), which can promote to a Crown Prince (太子, kōtaishi). Every other rule, including captures returning to hand and drops, is exactly as in Shogi.

Drunk Elephant

The Drunk Elephant moves one square in any direction except straight backward. It starts in front of the King.

Crown Prince

On reaching, moving within, or leaving the promotion zone (the three ranks furthest from you), a Drunk Elephant may promote to a Crown Prince. The Crown Prince moves one square in any direction, like a King.

Note: in the historical Shō Shogi the Crown Prince is a second royal piece that must also be captured to win. This implementation keeps a single royal King (as in this collection's Chu Shogi): the Prince is a strong King-stepping piece but is not itself royal.

Drops

Unlike historical Shō Shogi, captured pieces go to your hand and may be dropped, exactly as in Shogi. A captured Crown Prince reverts to an unpromoted Drunk Elephant in hand.
