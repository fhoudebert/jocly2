Kōtaishi Shogi

Kōtaishi Shogi is a Shogi variant that adds a Drunk Elephant (酔象). It can be promoted to a Crown Prince (太子, kōtaishi).

Drunk Elephant

The Drunk Elephant moves one square in any direction except directly backward. It starts on the square directly in front of the King.

Crown Prince

When entering, moving within, or leaving the promotion zone (the three farthest ranks), a Drunk Elephant may be promoted to a Crown Prince. The Crown Prince moves one square in any direction, just like a King.

This variant is inspired by the historical Shō Shogi (16th century). As in that game, the Crown Prince is a second royal piece that must also be captured in order to win.

Drops

Unlike historical Shō Shogi, captured pieces may be dropped back onto the board, exactly as in Shogi. A captured Crown Prince reverts to an unpromoted Drunk Elephant while in hand.
