Differences from orthodox chess

Initial setup

Played on a 5x5 board. Three arrangements have been proposed, and the game opens by asking which one you want to play; the choice counts as the first move and is remembered for the next game.

Gardner

The 1969 original, by Martin Gardner. Each player has 5 Pawns, 1 Rook, 1 Knight, 1 Bishop, 1 Queen and 1 King, and the two back ranks are in the same file order, so the Kings face each other on the e-file.

Baby

The same pieces, with Black's back rank mirrored rather than translated.

Malett

Jeff Mallett's asymmetric arrangement, where the two armies are not the same:

  • White: 5 Pawns, 1 Rook, 1 Queen, 1 King and 2 Knights
  • Black: 5 Pawns, 1 Rook, 1 Queen, 1 King and 2 Bishops
The Kings start on the c-file, and castling accordingly lands the King on b rather than c.

Castling: yes, in all three.

"En passant" capture: yes.

Double move for Pawns (start): yes when possible.
