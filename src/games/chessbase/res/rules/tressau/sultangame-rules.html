Sultan's Game, Tressau, 1840

Differences from orthodox chess

Played on a 11x11 board, 3 fairy pieces:

  • The Commander (aka Amazon): moves and captures like Queen + Knight

  • The Adjutant (aka Archbishop, aka Cardinal): moves and captures like Bishop + Knight

  • The Marshall: moves and captures like Rook + Knight

Inital setup

Each player starts with:

  • 11 Pawns
  • 2 Rooks
  • 2 Knights
  • 2 Bishops
  • 1 Commander
  • 1 Adjutant
  • 1 Marshall
  • 1 Queen
  • 1 king

Castling: yes.

"En passant" capture: yes.

Triple move for Pawns (start): yes.

Pawns promote to captured pieces only.
