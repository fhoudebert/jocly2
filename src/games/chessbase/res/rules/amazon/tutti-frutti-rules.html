Tutti frutti, by Ralph Betza and Philip Cohen, 1978-79.

Differences from orthodox chess

Played on a classical 8x8 board, 3 fairy pieces:

  • The Amazon: moves and captures like Queen + Knight

  • The Empress (aka Marshhal): moves and captures like Rook + Knight

  • The Princess (aka Cardinal): moves and captures like Bishop + Knight

Inital setup

Each player starts with:

  • 8 Pawns
  • 1 Rook
  • 1 Knight
  • 1 Bishop
  • 1 Empress
  • 1 Princess
  • 1 Amazon
  • 1 Queen
  • 1 king

Castling: yes.

"En passant" capture: yes.

Double move for Pawns (start): yes.
