Amazon chess

Differences from orthodox chess

The Queen is replaced by a fairy piece called Amazon which moves and captures like Knight + Queen

Inital setup

Each player starts with:

  • 8 Pawns
  • 2 Rooks
  • 2 Knights
  • 2 Bishops
  • 1 Amazon
  • 1 king

Castling: yes.

"En passant" capture: yes.

Double move for Pawns (start): yes.
