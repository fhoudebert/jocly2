Gustav III (or Shakspel)

This chess variant was invented by King Gustav III of Sweden (1746-92).

It is is a strange chess variant adding 4 Amazons on 4 extra squares in the corners of the board.

Played on a 8x8+4 board, 1 fairy piece:

  • The Amazon: moves and captures like Queen + Knight

Inital setup

Each player starts with:

  • 8 Pawns
  • 2 Rooks
  • 2 Knights
  • 2 Bishops
  • 2 Amazons
  • 1 Queen
  • 1 king

Castling: yes.

"En passant" capture: yes.

Double move for Pawns (start).
