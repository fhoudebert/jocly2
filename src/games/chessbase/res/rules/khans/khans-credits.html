Rules

Khan's Chess was designed by Couch Tomato (2023-2024), as a rebalanced successor to his own Orda Chess.

Jocly implementation

  • Model, view and rules: built on the chessbase module of Jocly by Michel Gutierrez (@_mig_) and Jérôme Choain (@jcfrog)
  • Piece symbols: Wikipedia fairy chess sprite set, as used by the other fairy variants of the module
  • The movement diagrams of the rules page are generated from the game model itself (see tests/khans/diagrams.test.js), so they cannot disagree with what the game plays

Expert level

The Expert level runs Fairy-Stockfish by Fabian Fichter (GPL-3.0), declaring Khan's Chess through the variant definition published in that project's own variants.ini.
