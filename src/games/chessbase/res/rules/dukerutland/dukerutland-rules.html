Duke of Rutland Chess

This 14x10 variants was invented by John Manners, 3rd Duke of Rutland (England) in 1747. Two extra fairy pieces are used: the Concubine (Knight+Rook) and the Crowned Rook (King+Rook).

Played on a 14x10 board, 2 fairy pieces:

  • The Concubine: moves and captures like Knight + Rook

  • The Crowned Rook: moves and captures like King + Rook

Inital setup

Each player starts with:

  • 14 Pawns
  • 2 Rooks
  • 2 Crowned Rooks
  • 3 Knights
  • 4 Bishops
  • 1 Concubine
  • 1 Queen
  • 1 king

Castling: no.

"En passant" capture: yes.

Triple move for Pawns (start).
