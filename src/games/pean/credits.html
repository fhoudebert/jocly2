Inspired by Heraklios by François Marcela-Froideval (Jeux & Stratégie, 1980). A tribute to its author; rules, map and tables entirely rewritten.

Only genre ideas are taken from it (hexes, zones of control, combat odds, cavalry charge, missile fire, victory on points), which belong to the common stock of wargaming. Neither the rules text, nor the map, the tables or the unit values are reproduced.

Historical sources: Xenophon, Hellenica, VI (Leuctra); Herodotus, Histories, IX (Plataea).

Jocly module (rules, scenarios, AI, view) and the "Attic pottery" and "Map" skins, entirely drawn by the program: jocly2, under the AGPL-3.0 licence.

"Insignia" skin: emblems and banners from 0 A.D. (Wildfire Games), under the CC BY-SA 3.0 licence, unmodified (res/images/ folder).

"Insignia" skin: terrain pictograms from The Battle for Wesnoth, by Emilien Rotival (LordBob), under the GNU GPL v2 or later (res/images/terrain/ folder).

"Tiles" skin: terrain tiles from The Battle for Wesnoth by J.W. Bjerk (Eleazar), Richard Kettering (Jetrel), Lari Nieminen (zookeeper), Hogne Håskjold (freim), Tommy (yobbo), Moritz Göbelbecker (mog), doofus-01, blarumyrran and other contributors, under the GNU GPL v2 or later (res/images/tuiles/ folder; per-file details in extra/wesnoth/README.md).

Laurion: the mine tile combines three Wesnoth tiles (dry hills by J.W. Bjerk, crater and rocks by doofus-01), GPL v2+; its pictogram is the cave entrance by Emilien Rotival (LordBob). Sources: Thucydides, VII, 19-27 (Decelea).

Unit portraits ("portrait" counter style): 0 A.D. (Wildfire Games), CC BY-SA 3.0, scaled down to 96 px (res/images/portraits/).
