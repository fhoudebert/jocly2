Inspired by Heraklios by François Marcela-Froideval (Jeux & Stratégie, 1980). A tribute to its author; rules, map and tables entirely rewritten.

Only genre ideas are taken from it (hexes, zones of control, combat odds, cavalry charge, missile fire, victory on points), which belong to the common stock of wargaming. Neither the rules text, nor the map, the tables or the unit values are reproduced.

Historical sources: Xenophon, Hellenica, VI (Leuctra); Herodotus, Histories, IX (Plataea).

Jocly module (rules, scenarios, AI, view) and the "Attic pottery" and "Map" skins, entirely drawn by the program: jocly2, under the AGPL-3.0 licence.

"Insignia" skin: emblems and banners from 0 A.D. (Wildfire Games), under the CC BY-SA 3.0 licence, unmodified (res/images/ folder).
