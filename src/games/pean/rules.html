Object

Two armies of Greek antiquity fight on a hex map. No dice: combat is resolved by a fixed table, and the only uncertainty is your opponent. The game ends when an army has lost half its value (it breaks), when an army is wiped out, or after the scenario's last turn: points are then counted.

The turn

On its turn a side gives orders, then fights combats. It has 4 orders, plus 1 per general in play (a scenario may change these numbers). An order activates one unit, or a group: contiguous units of the same type, which move together and keep their formation. A unit receives at most one order per turn. Once a combat is fought, no more movement.

Movement

Each unit has a movement allowance; each hex costs according to its terrain. Friendly units may be passed through; one unit per hex.

Zones of control

The six hexes around a unit are its zone of control (not across a river). A unit entering an enemy zone stops there. A unit starting its turn in an enemy zone is engaged: it cannot move, except light cavalry, which may disengage (but not move directly from one enemy zone to another). An engaged phalanx may still pivot.

Combat

Several units may attack the same enemy together; a unit attacks and is attacked at most once per turn. Divide the total attack by the defence (terrain included), rounding in the defender's favour:

OddsResult
below 1:1attack not allowed
1:1exchange: each side loses one step
3:2defender retreats one hex (if it cannot, it loses a step)
2:1defender loses a step and retreats
3:1 or moredefender eliminated

Each unit has two steps: reduced, its strength is halved (rounded up). Retreats are automatic, towards the side's own map edge, onto a free hex outside enemy zones of control. An attacker in contact may advance into the vacated hex. A scenario may supply its own table.

Abilities

  • Missile (archers, slingers, horse archers): attacks a target exactly 2 hexes away, if an intermediate hex is empty and does not block sight. Missile units never lose steps when attacking; on their own their result never exceeds a retreat. They do not fight in contact.
  • Charge (heavy cavalry): after a run of at least 3 hexes in a straight line its strength is doubled against an adjacent target — never against a phalanx from the front, uphill, into forest or marsh, or across a river.
  • Phalanx: +1 for each friendly phalanx lined up directly behind it along the axis of the combat (+2 at most), in attack and defence — except in forest or marsh. With the facing option a phalanx has a front (three sides, shown by a chevron): attacked in flank or rear, its defence is halved. It faces the direction of its last step; changing facing costs 1 movement point.
  • General: gives one more order while in play; losing him is costly.

Victory

A side's points = value of destroyed enemy units (a lost step counts pro rata) + points of the objectives it controls (last side to occupy it). An objective marked "for" a side scores only for that side. A margin at or below the scenario's threshold is a draw.

Input

Click a unit, then its destination (highlighted hexes; hover to see the path). "Whole line" selects the contiguous group of the same type; "Group" lets you add or remove a unit by clicking it. For a phalanx, pick its facing on arrival, or "Pivot in place". Click an enemy unit framed in red to prepare a combat: the odds and the result are shown before you resolve it; click your units to adjust the attackers. "End turn" sends everything.

Notation: D11-D8 order, 8:D11-D8 group of 8, D9/NE pivot, 3xE7+ three attackers on E7, advancing.
