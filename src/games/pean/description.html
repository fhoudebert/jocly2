Pean is a two-player tactical hex wargame with no luck and full information, set in Greece during the Persian and Peloponnesian wars. Hoplites in phalanx, peltasts, archers, light and heavy cavalry, generals: each turn you give a limited number of orders to units or whole lines, then fight combats whose outcome you know before committing to them.

The engine is generic: each battle is a scenario (map, armies, objectives, options). Three come with the game: an open battle in which each side buys and deploys its army, Leuctra (371 BC) and Plataea (479 BC).

A paean is a solemn song to Apollo, sung before battle and after victory.
